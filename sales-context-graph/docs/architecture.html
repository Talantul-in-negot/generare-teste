Architecture — as implemented

Sales Context Graph

Three untrusted-shaped sources — Salesforce, Gong, Showpad — become tenant-isolated, evidence-backed Claim nodes in Neo4j, then a bounded, scored selection served to seller, review and readiness workflows. Application-owned workflow state stays separate from evidence; nothing is asserted without a source segment and character span.

Tenant isolation, structurally enforced

Not a convention — GraphExecutor.tenant_query() rejects any Cypher whose matched nodes aren't scoped by workspace_id, including full-text and vector procedure calls.

Application-owned workflow and governance plane

Evidence remains evidence: reviews, readiness, Buyer Spaces and approved actions are separate tenant-scoped workflow records, never invented CRM facts.

Human Review
Review Console loads bounded candidates, persists reviewer assignment and SLA due date, then records the decision and targeted Claim reconciliation. Assignment/decision history remains inspectable.
Readiness
Curricula, assignments, knowledge checks, manager-scored role plays, coaching, certifications, learning resources and cohort summaries live in the workflow repository.
Buyer Space
A revocable invitation token begins in the buyer page URL fragment, moves to session storage, and is sent only as X-Buyer-Token. Portal views and uploads become engagement events.
Binary uploads
Bytes remain outside Neo4j. The upload boundary requires a ClamAV INSTREAM scan, writes only hash/object-key metadata to Neo4j, and rejects unavailable or unsafe scans before storage.
Retention + hold
A scheduler-invoked retention sweep removes expired application-owned Buyer Space upload bytes and metadata content, unless the Buyer Space is under a legal hold.
Operations
Prometheus metrics, FastAPI/Neo4j OpenTelemetry spans, local Prometheus/Alertmanager/Grafana, backup/restore and scheduled deterministic failure drills provide local evidence; managed production operations remain deployment work.
Dependency direction

One-way. api/ depends on everything below it; src/domain depends on nothing in this repo — no Neo4j import, no I/O.

FastAPI routes — parse request, call a use case, shape response. No business logic.

deterministic rules, candidate generation, scoring, decision policy, async human review.

AccessContext policy, OIDC/JWKS validation, rate limiting, audit, telemetry, caching and readiness.
src/storage/
Fail-closed binary Buyer Space boundary: bounded bytes, ClamAV scan, hash/object key metadata and retention deletion.
worker
Redis delivery with retries, visibility timeout, DLQ recovery and bounded concurrency per claim slot.
src/graph/
tenant-safe execution modes, schema/indexes, migrations, one repository per aggregate.
src/domain/
pure Pydantic v2 contracts + deterministic ID hashing. Nothing depends on it; it depends on nothing.
