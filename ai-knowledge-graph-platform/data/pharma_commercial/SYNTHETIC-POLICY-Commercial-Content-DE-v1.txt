Commercial content may be used only when its controlled version is approved,
current on the interaction date, and explicitly scoped to the requested product,
synthetic indication, market, and HCP specialty. Content marked expired, draft,
or withdrawn must not be used. A record without a controlled evidence reference
must be escalated for human review.
